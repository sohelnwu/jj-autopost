# Privacy Policy — JJ AutoPost

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

JJ AutoPost is a private automation tool operated by **JJ Network** (Sohel Rana, Ullapara, Sirajganj, Bangladesh). It is used only to publish content to Facebook Pages that JJ Network itself owns and manages (such as "JJ News" and "JJ Official").

## What data the app uses
- **Page access tokens** for JJ Network's own Facebook Pages, stored encrypted as GitHub Actions secrets. They are used only to publish posts, photos and videos to those Pages.
- **Public data** used to create content: public news headlines (titles and outlet names), public weather forecasts and calculated prayer times.

## What the app does NOT do
- It does not log in other users, and does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of Facebook users or Page followers.
- It does not read private messages, comments or profile information.
- It does not use cookies or tracking.

## Data retention and deletion
The app stores no user data. Page tokens can be revoked at any time by the Page owner in Facebook Settings → Business integrations, or by removing the app. To request deletion of any data or ask a question, contact JJ Network through the "JJ Official" Facebook Page.
